## 2. Hα, quenching and host environment

At 175 kpc the common-slope baseline differences for all satellites are −8.0 pp [−17.7, +1.7] for historical Hα, −6.3 pp [−15.4, +2.8] for exact-spectrum Hα, and −0.6 pp [−9.3, +8.2] for quenching. These models use 618, 659 and 653 galaxies in 225, 232 and 231 hosts. The corresponding separate-slope estimates are −3.5, −4.1 and +3.0 pp, all with intervals spanning zero.

For 10 ≤ log stellar mass < 10.5, exact-spectrum Hα gives **−16.0 pp** [−30.6, −1.5] with a common slope and **−23.3 pp** [−39.2, −7.4] with separate slopes (62 CG4 and 196 Control4C satellites across 174 hosts). The historical estimates are −15.5 [−31.1, +0.1] and −20.8 [−38.5, −3.2]. On the common complete sample, adding the aperture proxy moves the common-slope Hα estimate from −15.4 to −18.2 pp. With aperture adjustment and BPT filtering, the estimates are **−14.4 pp** [−30.6, +1.7] after Kewley AGN exclusion and **−9.5 pp** [−26.5, +7.4] after excluding composites as well. The three aperture samples contain 55/172, 36/134 and 29/94 CG4/Control4C satellites. Quenching gives +3.9 pp [−11.8, +19.6] with a common slope and +18.2 pp [+1.4, +35.1] with separate slopes (59/196 galaxies, 173 hosts).

The intermediate-mass compactness match gives a paired exact Hα difference of **−28.2 pp** [−50.0, −6.4] in 26 pairs (79 galaxies, 52 hosts) and a quenching difference of **+24.0 pp** [+2.0, +46.0] in 25 pairs (73 galaxies, 50 hosts). All-satellite paired baselines give −1.6 pp for exact Hα and −2.5 pp for quenching, both with intervals spanning zero. Intermediate-mass contrasts depend on the dispersion caliper, slopes and BPT selection. The morphology-adjusted matched quenching interaction contrast after Kauffmann exclusion changes from +48.2 pp on 11 original-label pairs to +9.2 pp on 13 frozen-label pairs, with wide intervals in both analyses. With the additional dispersion caliper, 16 frozen fits on the restricted intermediate-mass Kauffmann samples have singular matrices. ## Reproduction and inference

Run from the repository root.

    PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 .venv_clean/bin/python -u results/referee_freeze_20261120/run.py

The script loads source from commit d768251 via git archive, and reads the local caches. The median of the six pair separations, med_Rij, uses Planck15 angular-diameter distance at the mean member redshift for Table 1. Their arithmetic mean, mean_Rij, uses Planck15 angular-diameter distance at the median member redshift, following the earlier compactness audit. Both are proper kpc. N_Lim is the parent Lim catalogue multiplicity. N_Lim_Tempel counts cross-matched members. Host M200c is from the parent PC group table. [Input hashes and software](provenance.json) · [Geometry](tables/02_quartet_geometry.csv)

Regressions cluster by physical Lim host. The CG4–Control4C window estimates standardize to locally supported CG4 covariates and models adding host variables require local overlap on those variables as well. RG4 comparisons standardize to observed RG4 covariates within its shared radius range. The CSVs distinguish fit counts from local standardization counts. Paired estimates resample connected components, linking repeated physical hosts across arms. Table 5 uses 9,999 draws and compactness matching uses 4,999. Adjusted matched contrasts use 4,999 component-score bootstrap draws, with an inverse-Hessian update and nonlinear probability predictions at the fitted reference cohort. The coefficient SEs retain physical-host clustering. Intervals are pointwise.

The aperture models use a common sample with both EW joins, valid total sSFR, E/S class and a positive Petrosian half-light radius. The matched adjusted fits also require both quartets to contribute at least one complete observation. BPT exclusion uses the historical classifications for historical Hα and quenching and the repository curves applied to positive stored-spectrum fluxes for exact Hα. Unclassified BPT rows stay eligible. Fixed-mass bin estimates use bin intercepts and a common mass slope within each sample. Figure lines evaluate separate sample-specific logistic models at log mass 10.35 over the central 90% of quartet separations. In panel (b) the three symbols to the right of the dividing line denote comparison summaries at categorical positions.
