# T4 summary — pairwise tidal index: definition, common support, wording

Sect. 3.6 now states the exact construction extracted from the code: T_i = Σ_j M⋆,j/R_ij³ sums over the galaxy's *selected quartet co-members only* (not the full Lim host), with no velocity window, R_ij from the angular-diameter distance at the quartet's median redshift, co-members with unmeasured mass contributing zero (a lower bound), quartets missing any coordinate/redshift dropped whole, and — now stated explicitly — models that adjust only for stellar mass and satellite status on the pooled, non-deduplicated control catalogue with physical-group clustering. Common-support diagnostics are reported: the CG4 and control log T_i distributions overlap with coefficient 0.54, but medians differ by ≈1.1 dex and 15% of CG4 galaxies lie above the pooled-control 95th percentile, while collinearity is mild (point-biserial r = 0.18; VIF of the CG4 indicator 1.03). Sensitivity refits show the residual CG4 elliptical coefficient at fixed T_i is not robust: OR = 1.39 (linear, p = 0.03), 1.40 (natural cubic spline), 1.27 (deduplicated pool, p = 0.12), and 1.22 (trimmed to common support, p = 0.20), reinforcing the manuscript's conditional-attenuation (not mediation) reading. The Sect. 4.3 fibre-collision sentence is softened as requested: the 19%/6% sub-55″ fractions demonstrate differential crowding among *catalogued* galaxies, a downward bias of T_i from spectroscopically missing neighbours is labelled plausible but not demonstrated, and a photometric-companion index is deferred to future work.
