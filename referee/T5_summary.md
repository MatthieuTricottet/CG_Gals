# T5 summary — symmetric extreme-assignment bounds for missing sSFR

Appendix B now bounds every raw quenched-fraction contrast by the four extreme assignments in which all missing-sSFR galaxies on each side are set to quenched or star-forming, with consistent all-galaxy denominators on both sides (CG4 is never compared against a classified-only control fraction). Framed as the referee suggests: differential missingness is negligible against Control4B and Control4C (7.3% of CG4 members versus 6.4% and 6.8%) and material only against RG4 (2.2%). The raw RG4 quenched contrast survives all four corners — the all-member difference stays between +0.12 and +0.21 around the observed +0.17, and the satellite-only difference between +0.16 and +0.23 — while the small Control4B/4C differences (≤0.06) can change sign only under the doubly adversarial corner and are not the basis of any claim. The new appendix table lists all six comparisons (three controls × all-member/satellite-only) with per-side missing fractions and both corners.
