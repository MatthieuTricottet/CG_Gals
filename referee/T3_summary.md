# T3 summary — σ_v documentation and no-σ_v sensitivity

Sect. 2.4 documents that sigma_v is the quartet-level gapper estimate from the four selected members in every sample, including Control4B and Control4C quartets embedded in richer hosts. A Gaussian Monte Carlo gives a median sigma_gapper/sigma_true of 0.96 and a 16--84% range of 0.57--1.42 for four tracers. Sigma_v is complete for every quartet and therefore enters the relevant adjusted models and matches, but all such analyses were also repeated without it using the same samples, clustering, resampling, and seeds. The morphology odds ratios change little (for example, Control4B all-member OR 2.14 to 2.22 and Control4C 1.67 to 1.68). The galaxy-level match retains 234 CG4 galaxies and its elliptical-fraction difference changes from 0.139 to 0.155. Group-level satellite differences remain positive and Control4C remains the weakest comparison (0.28, 0.10, and 0.25 without sigma_v). Some raw p-values move across 0.05, notably the RG4 quenching sensitivity, but the estimates are not promoted to new findings because this is an alternate-covariate robustness check. The paper's conclusions do not depend on sigma_v. Full table: `referee/T3_no_sigma_table.csv`; values: `referee/values/T3.json`.
