# T1 summary — adjusted per-control families after the 55″ exclusion

We refit the unique Sect. 3.2 outcomes with the same covariates and cluster-robust uncertainty after excluding every galaxy whose nearest projected same-group neighbour lies within 55 arcsec. This removes 19% of CG4 members, compared with 6%, 10%, and 5% of Control4B, Control4C, and RG4. The all-member elliptical odds ratios change only from 2.14 to 2.16, 1.67 to 1.71, and 2.81 to 2.71, respectively; the corresponding 95% confidence intervals remain well above unity. The satellite morphology result is similarly stable, while the BGG estimates remain imprecise. Thus the closest-pair classifications do not account for the elliptical excess. The full side-by-side results are in `referee/T1_crowding_table.csv` and `referee/values/T1.json`.
