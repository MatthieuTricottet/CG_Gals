# T1 summary — adjusted per-control families after the 55″ exclusion

We agree that robustness of the adjusted estimand must be shown on the adjusted estimand, and we now refit the complete Sect. 3.2 per-control adjusted families (all eight outcomes, identical covariates, cluster-robust SEs by physical Lim group, Holm within each contrast as a separately labelled sensitivity family) on the sample excluding every galaxy whose nearest projected same-group neighbour lies within 55″ — removing 19% of CG4 members versus 6%, 10%, and 5% of Control4B, Control4C, and RG4. Every Holm-significant published result persists: the all-member elliptical odds ratio moves from 2.14 to 2.16 against Control4B, 1.67 to 1.71 against Control4C, and 2.81 to 2.71 against RG4 (Holm p ≤ 0.0044 in all cases), with the satellite-only elliptical, spiral-deficit, and Control4B quenched families equally stable. No null result becomes significant, and the only visible movement is the (never-significant) RG4 quenched-satellite contrast drifting further from significance. Sect. 3.5 now reports this adjusted persistence alongside the raw diagnostic, with the full side-by-side table in `referee/T1_crowding_table.csv` and values in `referee/values/T1.json`.
