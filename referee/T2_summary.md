# T2 summary — within-host models with and without host-centric radius

We refit the Sect. 3.4 conditional-logit and fixed-effect GLM comparisons for elliptical morphology and quenching without the projected host-centric radius term, using the same complete-case samples and host strata. With radius, the elliptical odds ratios are 1.47 (95% CI 0.92--2.35; p = 0.108) and 1.57 (1.02--2.43; p = 0.041); without radius they rise to 1.90 (1.23--2.95; p = 0.004) and 2.06 (1.36--3.12; p = 0.00064). The quenched estimates remain broad and compatible with no difference in all four fits. Conditioning on radius asks whether compact-subconfiguration members differ beyond their more central location, whereas omitting it includes that location difference. The attenuation therefore supports the local-density interpretation rather than indicating an estimator conflict. Full grid: `referee/T2_within_host_table.csv`; values: `referee/values/T2.json`.
