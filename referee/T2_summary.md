# T2 summary — within-host models with and without host-centric radius

We refit the Sect. 3.4 conditional logit and FE-GLM (elliptical; quenched) without the projected host-centric radius term, on the identical complete-case sample and strata, and now report both p-value conventions for both estimators (Holm across the two outcomes within each estimator, the published family convention). With radius, the elliptical odds ratios are 1.47 (conditional logit; Holm p = 0.216) and 1.57 (FE-GLM; nominal p = 0.041, Holm p = 0.083) — under the like-for-like correction the two estimators agree, resolving the apparent 0.216-versus-0.041 inconsistency. Without radius the elliptical excess is strong in both estimators: OR = 1.90 (Holm p = 0.008) and 2.06 (Holm p = 0.0013), while quenched remains null in all four cells. As Sect. 3.4 now explains, the radius-conditioned model asks whether CG members differ *beyond central location* and the radius-free model *including location*; under the paper's local-density interpretation the conditioned coefficient is expected to be strongly attenuated, so the positive radius-free coefficient supports the location reading rather than contradicting the published within-host null.
