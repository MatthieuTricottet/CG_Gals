# T6 summary — origin of the Control4C satellite-mass offset, and rewording

The +0.18 dex CG4-Control4C satellite-mass offset raised by the referee was dominated by the Control4C construction defect found in T0. In the defective unrestricted sample the raw offset is +0.27 dex, largely because of Delta m > 3 interlopers with mean log Mstar = 9.42; in the corrected sample it falls to +0.01 dex raw and +0.03 dex (SMD 0.09) in the matched pairs. Within the shared Delta m <= 3 window, distance-ranked selection samples the eligible-satellite magnitude distribution evenly (Control4C KS D = 0.04; CG4 D = 0.06, p = 0.44), whereas brightness-ranked Control4B is strongly weighted toward luminous satellites (D = 0.30; mean satellite log Mstar 0.21 dex above CG4). The manuscript now presents the original and satellite-mass-balanced group matches as distinct physical comparisons. In the current fits, the CG4 minus control smooth-satellite fractions are +0.160, +0.114, and +0.196 for Control4B, Control4C, and RG4; the mass-balanced variants are +0.198, +0.086, and +0.256. The weaker Control4C difference supports the interpretation that compact groups occupy the dense end of ordinary-group projected cores, while present-day stellar mass may itself contain part of the environmental history.
