# T6 summary — origin of the Control4C satellite-mass offset, and rewording

The +0.18 dex CG4−Control4C satellite-mass offset the referee asked about was, we now find, dominated by the Control4C construction defect uncovered in T0: on the defective (unrestricted) sample the raw offset is +0.27 dex, of which the Δm > 3 interlopers (mean log M⋆ = 9.42) account for essentially all — on the corrected sample the offset collapses to +0.01 dex raw and +0.03 dex (SMD 0.09) in the matched pairs. The referee's proposed mechanism is confirmed where it applies: within the shared Δm ≤ 3 window, distance-ranked selection samples the eligible-satellite magnitude-gap distribution democratically (Control4C: KS D = 0.04 against the parent-group reference; CG4 itself: D = 0.06, p = 0.44), whereas brightness-ranked Control4B is strongly bright-weighted (D = 0.30, p ~ 10⁻¹¹⁵; mean satellite log M⋆ higher than CG4's by 0.21 dex) — the one amendment being that CG4's own satellites sample the window democratically rather than bright-weighted. Sect. 3.3 is rewritten as requested: the with- and without-mass-balance matches are presented as different estimands (composition at matched group scale versus at matched satellite mass), not ordered levels of bias correction and not a bracketing of a causal effect, with a new Sect. 4.1 caution that present-day M⋆ may itself encode the environmental history under test. The abstract, Sect. 3.2, Sect. 4.1, and the conclusion now carry the corrected-sample formulation: a strong adjusted excess against Control4B and RG4, a smaller but detected excess against Control4C, and a group-level composition hierarchy (significant/significant/marginal) that is stable under mass balancing — consistent with compact groups sampling the extreme local-projected-density tail of ordinary-group cores.
