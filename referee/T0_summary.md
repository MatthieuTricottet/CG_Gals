# T0 summary — Control4C construction audit and repair

Prompted by the referee's question, we audited the Control4C sample against the full parent catalogue and found that the CSV inherited from our Paper I data release selected the three nearest projected companions *regardless of relative magnitude*: 457 of 705 groups (31.7% of companions) contained a companion with Δm > 3 (up to 5.76 mag), violating the intended construction. The sample behind Paper I's *published* statistics did apply the Δm ≤ 3 filter — re-deriving Control4C with the documented "filter first, then rank" construction reproduces that paper's published counts (61 exclusions → 704 groups) and every published Control4C statistic (Table 2 medians; Table 3 T₁/T₂) exactly, so the defect lay in the distributed data file, not in the published analysis. We have therefore regenerated Control4C with the Δm ≤ 3-restricted construction (`src/sample_construction.py`), which changes the quartet membership of 457 groups, and re-run the entire analysis pipeline on the corrected sample; machine-verified assertions (`referee/T0_control4c_audit.py`: every companion within 3 magnitudes, every quartet exactly the three nearest such members, zero violations) now gate the sample, and Sect. 2.1 states the eligibility filter explicitly. Control4B and RG4 pass the same assertions with zero violations by construction. The audit of the defective sample is preserved in `referee/T0_control4c_audit_shipped_FAIL.md`, and the resolved off-by-one (60 vs 61 exclusions) is documented in OPEN_QUESTIONS.md #1.
